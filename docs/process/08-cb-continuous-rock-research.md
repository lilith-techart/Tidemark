## Continuous geometry

**Problem.** Patches and simple envelopes repeatedly produced artificial bases and disconnected support relationships.

**Method / implementation.** Human-approved B2 intent establishes a thick continuous core, offset high point, stepped tiers and seven locked planning regions. Task-specific Blender/Python routines use controlled points and triangulated surfaces. R2 studies shoulders, R3 face flow, R4 coastline, and R5 combines bounded changes. These are not a general natural-geology generator.

**Measured result.** The historical final receipt records four submitted candidates as technical passes. R5 Revision2 independent review-package readback records mesh signature `7727a77b6a8d32ab4e35d0e5a892d7222a6ccb759c9bb7458c8a5ca1d75f4079`; review-ready Blend SHA256 `11b41300a338e26f53eb8d688bcf29baad9b8a00ac02769cc5100e46ad8e5655`.

**Limitations / next step.** Crystal-like facets, rectangular bearing areas and a polygonal submerged closure remain. Limited intersection checks are not a rigorous proof. Next: actual building proxy assembly and spatial review, without silently changing locked inputs.

## Foundation sampling is not certification

**Problem.** A front view can conceal unsupported edges and contact gaps.

**Method.** Compare actual generated surfaces to locked footprints; record hits, gaps and tolerance separately from collision/character tests.

**Measured result.** Historical receipt: **511/511 planning samples per candidate**, maximum absolute gap approximately **9.460449 × 10⁻⁶ m**. This is floating-point sampled contact, not a physical construction tolerance.

**Limitations / next step.** No actual buildings, complete bearing-area, load-bearing, collision or walkability certification. Lab Z=0 is not calibrated to UE ocean level. Next: actual foundation corners, entrances and adverse views after authorized assembly.

## Save / fresh reload / camera evidence

**Problem.** Script success does not prove the persisted scene is the reviewed one.

**Method.** Save a new candidate; end the authoring process; reopen from disk in an independent Blender process; compare geometry signatures and scene inventory; capture registered cameras.

**Measured result.** Historical `REVIEW_PROFILE_PERSISTENCE` new-process checks for R5 Revision2 are PASS. Eight cameras were captured at **1600 × 900**. Hero `CB_MainHero`: perspective **45 mm**, location **(44, −74, 36) m**. R1/R5 comparisons use recorded camera parameters, not pretend concept-image calibration.

**Limitations.** This publication rechecks historical evidence hashes; it does not rerun Blender. Rock-only reload does not prove six buildings were generated. New settlement composition remains resource-blocked.
